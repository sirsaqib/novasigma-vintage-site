Honest grading

What the grades mean

No mystery bales. Every piece is hand-checked and graded before it ships — what you see is what lands at your warehouse.

Grade A

Premium vintage

Excellent condition with minimal wear. Strong prints, working zips, no stains or holes. Ready to resell as-is.

  • Minimal to no visible wear
  • All buttons, zips & seams intact
  • Best for retail & online resale

Grade B

Great value

Light wear or minor flaws — small marks, light fading. Perfect for discount rails and high-volume sellers.

  • Light wear, minor flaws
  • Washed & wearable
  • Best margin per piece

Grade C

Recycling

Heavily worn pieces sold for textile recycling and wiping-rag production. Priced by weight, not by piece.

  • Heavy wear or damage
  • Sold per kilo
  • Recycling & rag trade

How it works

01

Sourcing

We buy unsorted vintage in bulk from Karachi's wholesale markets, including Sher Shah.

02

Sorting

Every piece is hand-sorted by category, brand and era by our trained team.

03

Grading

Each item is inspected and graded A, B or C against a fixed checklist.

04

Baling

Graded stock is compressed into 45kg export bales, weighed and labelled.

05

Shipping

Bales ship FOB Karachi by sea — 20 to 35 days to the USA, UK and EU.

T / T

Bank wire transfer. Standard terms: 30% advance with order, 70% before shipment. Fastest way to start.

L / C

FOB Karachi

All prices are FOB Karachi unless stated. CIF quotes to your nearest port available on request.

Lead time

Sea freight runs 20–35 days depending on destination. Bales are ready to ship within 7 days of payment.
